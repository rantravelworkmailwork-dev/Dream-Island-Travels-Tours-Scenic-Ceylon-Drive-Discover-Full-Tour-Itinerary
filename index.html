Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Sri Lanka and meet your private chauffeur at Bandaranaike International Airport at 10:50 PM. Enjoy a private transfer to your hotel in Negombo for check-in and a restful night after your flight.

🤝 Airport Pick-Up • 10:50 PM 🚗 Private Transfer to Negombo
Overnight: Negombo — Negombo Ocean Breeze – Bliss on the Beach Luxury Apartments (booked by guests)

Depart Negombo with your private chauffeur and travel to Sigiriya. En route, visit the historic Dambulla Cave Temple and the Golden Buddha Statue before checking in for your stay.

🛕 Dambulla Cave Temple 🛕 Golden Buddha Statue
Overnight: Sigiriya — La Cloudz

Embark on an exciting Habarana elephant safari. Optionally, unwind afterwards with a traditional Ayurvedic massage.

🐘 Habarana Elephant Safari 💆 Optional: Traditional Ayurvedic Massage
Overnight: Sigiriya — La Cloudz

Morning excursion to climb the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress. In the afternoon, visit the ancient ruins of the Polonnaruwa historical city.

🥾 Sigiriya Rock Fortress 🏛️ Polonnaruwa Ancient City
Overnight: Sigiriya — La Cloudz

Drive towards Kandy with a stop at a Matale Spice Garden en route. In the evening, visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic.

🌶️ Spice Garden 🛕 Temple of the Tooth
Overnight: Kandy — Rivora Heights

Explore the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya during the morning. Spend a relaxed afternoon in Kandy.

🌿 Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens
Overnight: Kandy — Rivora Heights

Scenic drive from Kandy to Nuwara Eliya. En route, visit a working tea factory and plantation, and admire the cascading Ramboda Falls.

☕ Tea Factory & Tea Plantation 🌊 Ramboda Waterfalls
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya — La Grand Villa

Start the day at Horton Plains National Park, then enjoy the world-renowned scenic train journey from Nuwara Eliya to Ella through rolling green hills and misty mountains.

🥾 Horton Plains National Park 🚂 Scenic Train Ride to Ella
Overnight: Ella — The Palace Eco Cottage (booked by guests)

Explore Ella's highlights including a hike up Little Adam's Peak and a visit to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge. Spend the rest of the day relaxing in Ella.

🥾 Little Adam's Peak 🌉 Nine Arch Bridge
Overnight: Ella — The Palace Eco Cottage (booked by guests)

Travel from Ella to Yala, stopping along the way to witness the stunning Ravana Falls.

🌊 Ravana Falls 🚗 Scenic Drive
Overnight: Yala / Tissa — Tamarind Tree

Early morning wildlife safari at Yala National Park. Afterward, travel to the historic city of Galle.

🐆 Yala National Park Safari (Morning) 🚗 Drive to Galle
Overnight: Galle — Olinda (booked by guests)

Enjoy a day of snorkelling along the southern coast.

🤿 Snorkelling
Overnight: Galle — Olinda (booked by guests)

Drive from Galle to Colombo and spend the rest of the day relaxing at leisure.

🚗 Drive to Colombo 🌴 Day at Leisure
Overnight: Colombo — Granbell Colombo

Enjoy a city tour around Colombo. In the evening, enjoy a private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight.

🏙️ Colombo City Tour ✈️ Night Airport Transfer
Departure Transfer: Private airport drop-off on the night of 18th January.
